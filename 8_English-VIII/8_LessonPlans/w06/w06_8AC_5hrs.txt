	* Wilbur, a runt pig, is saved from an early death by kind-hearted Fern, a young farm girl.
	  Wilbur is sold to Fern's uncle, Homer Zuckerman and begins life in Zuckerman's barn cellar.
	  Wilbur is a sensitive and overly dramatic soul. He is lonely in his new home even though the barn 
	  cellar is full of other creatures and a few notable characters like Templeton the rat, the Goose, 
	  and the Old Sheep. Wilbur is informed by the Old Sheep that he is being fattened for slaughter 
	  (Christmas dinner!).
	  
	  Enter Charlotte, a clever and compassionate spider who befriends Wilbur and courageously devises
	  a plan to save him. Charlotte spins flattering words into her web ("Some Pig", "Terrific", 
	  "Radiant") that suggest Wilbur is no ordinary pig. The Zuckermans and all of the townspeople fall 
	  for the trick and Wilbur is crated up for a special appearance at the County Fair. Charlotte and 
	  Templeton go along as stowaways.
	  
	  The events at Count Fair provide the climax of the story. Wilbur loses the blue ribbon but wins a 
	  special prize which saves his life. Fern, who had been continuously by Wilbur's side, is beginning 
	  to grow up. At the County Fair she is more entranced with riding the Ferris wheel with a boy named 
	  Henry Fussy than following the exploits of Wilbur, Charlotte, and Templeton. Charlotte builds her 
	  egg sac but cannot return to the barn cellar. Templeton rescues the egg sac with the promise of 
	  perpetual food and Wilbur endearingly takes the egg sac back home. Charlotte dies alone.
	  
	  Next spring the young spiders hatch. Wilbur is delighted but soon becomes desperately sad as all 
	  the young spiders seem to have floated away. Fortunately, three spiders remain and take up 
	  residence in their mother's doorway. Wilbur lives out the rest of his days in the wonderful barn 
	  cellar surrounded by generations of spiders, geese, and other friends.
