Final Exam [Due: Final Exam Week]

The final exam is a take-home exam. It consists solely in two tasks: i) generating a professional resume/CV, and ii) writing a cover letter for a job that you are interested in. Your resume/CV must be a professional document produced with the help of an Overleaf template. That is, your resume will be built in LaTex using the Overleaf website and downloaded as a .pdf file. It is recommended that you save a copy of the .tex file for your records. Your cover letter must be a professionally formatted document that follows the general guidelines for cover letters and is geared to a specific position at a specific organization. You may select a job post from an online job site or select a desired position from an organization that you are interested in working for.  Both documents must be written in English. For more information, see the attached document, "FinalExam_Marron_Ingles-X.pdf"


NB. Marron's modified APA format applies (see Class Materials in Teams). Copies must be submitted to Teams in PDF format or printed and given directly to the instructor. Students should save copies of all assignments in PDF format.

The Tao Te Ching
	-- Lao Tzu
##################
https://taoism.net/tao-te-ching-online-translation/
	
第七章				Chapter 7
天長地久				Heaven and Earth are everlasting
天地所以能長且久者		  The reason Heaven and Earth can last forever
以其不自生			Is that they do not exist for themselves
故能長生				Thus they can last forever
是以聖人				Therefore the sages:
後其身而身先			Place themselves last but end up in front
外其身而身存			Are outside of themselves and yet survive
非以其無私邪			Is it not due to their selflessness?
故能成其私			That is how they can achieve their own goals


第八章				Chapter 8
上善若水				The highest goodness resembles water
水善利萬物而不爭		Water greatly benefits myriad things without contention
處眾人之所惡		  It stays in places that people dislike
故幾於道			   Therefore it is similar to the Tao
居善地				Dwelling with the right location
心善淵				Feeling with great depth
與善仁				Giving with great kindness
言善信				Speaking with great integrity
正善治				Governing with great administration
事善能				Handling with great capability
動善時				Moving with great timing
夫唯不爭				Because it does not contend
故無尤				It is therefore beyond reproach
