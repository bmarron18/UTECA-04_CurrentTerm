	1. Who wrote the paper?
	2. What is the paper about?

--- Task 1
The first step in academic research is finding material related to your area of interest. This used to mean going to the library. Today, it's a matter of internet searching.
*Step 1
Search: "Han empire" y/o "Han dynasty" y/o "the Three Kingdoms period" in the following open-access sites:
	1. Google Scholar
		https://scholar.google.com/
	2. JSTOR
		https://www.jstor.org
		
	3. Open-Access Journals (pick one)
		a. Journal of Chinese History 中國歷史學刊 
			https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/open-access
				
		b. The Chinese Historical Review
			https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ytcr20
		
		c. Journal of the European Association for Chinese Studies
			https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/index
		
		d. USC Libraries, University of Southern California, Research Guides
		Chinese Studies *: Journals
			https://libguides.usc.edu/chinese_studies/journals
*Step 2
From each site download one (1) academic paper. Papers will typically be in the fields of
	Art
	Archeology
	Politics
	Philosophy
	Sociology
	History
	Other?
*Step 3
In your homework i) list the sources you used, and ii) list the citation for each of the three (3) papers. Citation must be in APA or MLA or Chicago style. Briefly describe your experience using each site.
*Step 4
Select one (1) of the papers for presentation at a future date. For next week, be prepared to tell the class which paper you selected and why.
--- Task 2
In the textbook, "Presentation Zen: Simple ideas on presentation design and delivery" (Reynolds, G. (2019), skim through the Introduction and read Seth Godin (p. 20) and In Sum (p. 25). Answer the following questions,
	1. Do you typically use PowerPoint (or a similar software) when you are asked to give a 
	presentation? Do you like giving presentations before groups of people?
	2. What questions should you be asking about presentation design and delivery that you have not 
	asked in the past? 
	3. Do you think that, in the past, you have adhered to the suggested guidelines for dynamic 
	storytelling in presentations? 
	4. What aspects of the design and delivery process may have caused “suffering” for your audiences 
	in the past?
The Reynolds textbook can be found in Teams, Class Materials (Textbook_Reynolds_PresentationZen_NewRider.pdf)
